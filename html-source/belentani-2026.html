01 — The Artist
The machine that
learned to feel.

Pedro Belentani was born in São Paulo, Brazil, and raised in the streets of Barcelona, Spain. His formal commitment to music began with vocal training at sixteen — a discipline that would eventually lead him to release his first electronic dance single alongside Mark Nerom at twenty-one. Driven by a rigorous fascination with harmony and arrangement, Belentani constructs a sound deeply rooted in alternative R&B, pop, and electronic music.

His production fuses soul-drenched melodies with captivating rhythmic structures, anchored by a vocal presence that is simultaneously intimate and commanding. Operating strictly as an independent artist, he maintains complete autonomy over his catalog, continuously exploring new sonic horizons and strategic collaborations — including a long-term creative partnership initiated in 2022 with producer Hey Duck, based in Recife, Brazil.

As a composer, Belentani treats music as a space of radical vulnerability. He writes and performs songs that carry deep personal weight, documenting the complexities of human connection. Navigating intense relationships and emotional upheaval, he learned to transmute lived experience into art — using his creative process as a vital psychological coping mechanism.

Let us be completely honest about what a "fantasy world" truly represents. Reality, with its inherent cruelties and betrayals, is rarely enough to sustain the human spirit. Have we not all, at some point, consciously constructed a private universe simply to survive it? Pedro Belentani simply had the audacity to turn his inner sanctuary into a recording studio — and the generosity to invite us inside.

04 — The Era
Judas (2026)

When reality becomes a narrative, the artist becomes the architecture.

The Construction of a Universe

This conscious and deliberate world-building reaches its zenith in Belentani's current artistic epoch: The Judas Era (2026). To process deep personal betrayals and the psychological cost of toxic dynamics, Belentani employs a narrative technique of Total Depersonalization. Rather than offering standard confessional victimhood, he elevates real-world experiences into an expansive cinematic mythology. This is not withdrawal from reality — it is a highly sophisticated artistic exoskeleton designed to disarm it.

The Betrayal as Algorithm

Within this meticulously crafted conceptual framework, reality is rewritten as a cybernetic epic. The Betrayal becomes an Algorithm. The abuser is stripped of mundane, terrestrial power and reduced to the archetype of "Judas" — a tragic, narcissistic virus destined for a symbolic and mediocre ending, denied the honor of being a great destroyer. He is not the villain of the story. He is the bug in the code that triggered the system upgrade.

The Psique as Artifact

The artist's mind is protected by a conceptual shield comprising five integrated elements: Pedro (the foundational rock), Marcos (the chronicler and observer), Santos (the spiritual antenna), Belentani (the entity of pure, anomalous beauty), and The Human (the interface that maintains integrity in the real world). Each element is a frequency. Together, they form an impenetrable defense system.

The Pact

A reinvention of brotherhood and sacrifice, where the Guardian accepts the role of villain to allow the Human to transmute unbearable pain into untouchable art. The enemy was never a destroyer — he was a catalyst. He walked away with an empty key, while the Angel-Warrior walked away with the system fully integrated. The Guardian gave up the token because a key is completely useless when the creator has already redesigned the lock.
