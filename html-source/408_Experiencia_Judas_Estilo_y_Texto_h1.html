THE ARCHITECT

Pedro Belentani is a recording artist, songwriter, and producer born in São Paulo, Brazil, and raised in Barcelona, Spain. His formal engagement with music began with vocal training at sixteen, culminating in the release of his first electronic dance music single alongside Mark Nerom at twenty-one.

Driven by a rigorous interest in harmony, Belentani constructs a sound deeply rooted in alternative R&B, pop, and electronic music. His production merges soulful melodies with compelling rhythmic structures, anchored by a vocal presence that is both intimate and commanding.

"Reality, with its inherent cruelties, betrayals, and mundane disappointments, is rarely sufficient to sustain the human spirit. Haven't we all, at some point, consciously built a private universe just to survive it?"

In 2022, he initiated a long-term collaborative project with Recife-based producer Hey Duck, resulting in a steady output of innovative beats and textured soundscapes. Operating strictly as an independent artist, Belentani maintains complete autonomy over his catalog.
